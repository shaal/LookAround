 A SMALL EXPERIMENT IN SPATIAL INPUT

A turn of the head.
A change of perspective.

Choose a sample or drop your own GLB, enable the camera, then face forward and look at the dot. Hold still for one second while the green ring fills to set your starting position.

Orbit object

Turn away from center to move around the object continuously. Hold a small turn to go all the way around; center your head to stop.

Head camera

Slide your head left/right or up/down to move the camera with it. Head turns don’t rotate the view. Gaze rotates the object by default; turn it off with Gaze rotates object.

Look around

Your camera stays in place. Turning your head looks left, right, up, and down. Return to neutral to look forward again.

Eyes and Both are experimental. Iris movement gives a rough directional input, not a calibrated gaze point. Head tracking is the recommended starting point. Use Invert horizontal if your camera's direction feels reversed.

Drag or scroll takes manual control. Space pauses movement. C sets a new neutral by reopening the dot without resetting the view. Lost tracking freezes the camera; returning to the camera asks you to hold still again. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
